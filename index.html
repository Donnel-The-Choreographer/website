Meet The Founder

About Donnel The Choreographer

Donnel is a professional choreographer based in Harare, Zimbabwe, specialising in wedding choreography, dance classes, music video choreography and creative dance concepts.

With a passion for excellence, creativity and confidence, Donnel has helped countless couples, beginners and artists transform their ideas into unforgettable performances.

From first rehearsal to final performance, Donnel walks with you step by step — patiently, professionally, and with joy — until you dance with confidence and grace.
